The story

Lac Bay raised a lot of us. Frans Paradise is how we share it.

The bay is flat, warm and shallow, with a steady trade wind. Generations of local kids learned to ride here, and some of them took it all the way to the world tour. The ones who stayed never stopped sharing it.

On 30 December 2018 the shack opened on the beach at Sorobon. It stays small on purpose. The same faces on the beach, a crew that knows your name, and a spot in the shade even when there's no wind. First timers, kids and riders chasing their next move, every day from 9 to 6.
