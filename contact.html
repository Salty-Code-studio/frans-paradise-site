Quick answers

Anything else? Just message us.

Can I book a lesson for today?

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you what's free today.

Do I need previous experience?

No. You start on the beach, then move into shallow, flat water. An instructor stays close the whole time.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, sunscreen, water and sunglasses with a strap. We have the rest.

How old do kids need to be?

Kids lessons start at 7. Ages 6 and under can try a 30 minute toddler session.

Can I store my own gear?

Yes. Book your spot in time, or send us a message.

Is there a rescue boat?

Yes, every day we're open. A rescue costs $10.
