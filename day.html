Sorobon, Lac Bay

A day at Frans Paradise

Trade winds, flat turquoise water and a shack full of good vibes. Here's how a day with us can go.

From 9 to 6

Come for a lesson, stay for the day. The shack is open every day.

9:00

Arrive at the shack

Head to Sorobon beach and find the painted container under the shade sails. The crew sorts your gear and your plan for the day.

Morning

Your lesson

It starts with a briefing on the beach, then out into the flat, shallow water of Lac Bay with your instructor close by.

Midday

Cool off in the shade

Rest under the sails, rehydrate and swap stories with other riders before the next session.

Afternoon

Ride on your own

Rent a board and practise what you learned, or bring your own gear. The rescue boat is out every day we're open.

18:00

Last run, then home

Catch the last wind, pick up a tee or a cap at the shack, and plan tomorrow's session.
