    Moments on Lac Bay

    How a quiet lagoon became a world stage.

    2002

    The first PWA freestyle event on Bonaire puts Lac Bay on the world map.

    2011

    PWA World Cup on Bonaire. Kiri Thode is crowned King of Lac Bay.

    2018

    Taty Frans opens Frans Paradise on Sorobon beach.

    2019

    Taty wins three Caribbean events, including the Défi Wind Caribbean.

    2023

    Youp Schmit wins King of the Caribbean at Frans Paradise.

    Today

    Arxon Gomperts and Tycho Smits train here and win on the PWA Youth tour.
