How a first lesson goes

No experience needed. You learn the basics on land before you get wet.

On the beach

You start on the sand: how the board and sail work, and how to stand.

Into shallow water

Then into the flat, shallow water of Lac Bay, the friendliest place to fall in.

On your board

You sail your first runs while your instructor stays close the whole time.

Included & what to bring

  • Your instructor and all the gear: board, sail or wing, and a vest.
  • Bring swimwear, sunscreen and water.
  • Sunglasses with a strap help a lot.
  • A rescue boat is on the water every day we're open.
