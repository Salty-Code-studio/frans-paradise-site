Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version

This website doesn't use cookies. We don't sell or share your details. You choose what you tell us, usually on WhatsApp. If you want something deleted, send us a message and we'll do it.

Who we are

Frans Paradise is a windsurf and wing foil school on the beach at Sorobon, Lac Bay, Bonaire. In this policy, "we" means Frans Paradise. You can reach us at info@fransparadise.com or on WhatsApp at +599 785 0514.

What we collect, and why

When you message us

The booking buttons on this site write a message for you and open WhatsApp. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp yourself. The website doesn't store what you picked or typed.

When you message, call or email us, we see what you send: usually your name, phone number, the day you want to come and things like your level or how many people are coming. We use this only to plan your lesson or rental and to answer your questions. WhatsApp is run by Meta, and their own privacy policy covers how they handle messages.

On the beach

For lessons and rentals we may write down your name, contact details and what you booked or rented. For kids we may note the age and a parent's contact. We keep this for running the school and for our bookkeeping.

Visiting this website

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages people use. It uses no cookies, doesn't build a profile of you and doesn't follow you to other sites. We only see totals, like "120 people looked at the rentals page this week".

Like every website, the server that hosts these pages briefly logs technical details such as your IP address and browser. This is needed to deliver the pages and keep them safe.

Other services this site uses

  • Wind forecast. The wind numbers come from Open-Meteo. Your browser asks them for the forecast for Lac Bay, so they see your IP address. No personal details are sent.
  • Google Maps. The map on the contact page only loads when you tap "Show the map". From then on Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Links out. Links to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Google reviews take you to those services. Their own policies apply there.

Our fonts and icons are stored on our own site, so loading a page doesn't send your details to other companies.

Photos and video

We sometimes take photos and video on the beach and share them on our website and Instagram. If you or your kids are in a shot and you'd rather not be, tell us. We'll leave it out or take it down.

How long we keep things

We keep booking details as long as we need them to run the school and as long as the law asks us to keep records for bookkeeping. WhatsApp chats stay in our phone until we delete them. Ask us and we'll delete yours sooner, unless the law says we must keep it.

Your rights

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also object to us using it. Send a message to info@fransparadise.com or on WhatsApp and we'll answer within a month. Bonaire follows the Dutch Caribbean privacy law (Wet bescherming persoonsgegevens BES). If you think we got something wrong, you can complain to the data protection authority, but we'd like the chance to fix it first.

Changes

If we change how we handle your details, we update this page and the date at the top.
