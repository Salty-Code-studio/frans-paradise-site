Coaching built around you

Every rider has different goals. Your instructor tailors each session to your level, your gear and what you want to get better at, with honest feedback on the water.

Lac Bay's steady trade winds and open, flat water make it a great place to push your limits while staying in a safe spot.

  • Perfect your gybes and transitions
  • Plane faster, ride in stronger wind
  • Land freestyle moves and tricks
  • Get up and stay up on the foil
  • Prepare for competition
