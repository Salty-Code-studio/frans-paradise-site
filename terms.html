Terms

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version

Book on WhatsApp, listen to your instructor, look after the gear, and tell us if you can't make it. We'll look after you on the water.

Who we are

These terms are for lessons, rentals and storage at Frans Paradise, on the beach at Sorobon, Lac Bay, Bonaire. You can reach us at info@fransparadise.com or on WhatsApp at +599 785 0514.

Lessons

  • A lesson is booked when we confirm the day and time with you, on WhatsApp or on the beach.
  • Prices are in US dollars and per person, as shown on this site and at the shack. The price we confirm with you is the one that counts.
  • Group lessons have at most 5 people. Kids lessons start from age 7.
  • You need to be comfortable in the water. Tell us about any health issue that could matter on the water before you start.
  • Kids under 18 need a parent or guardian to agree to the lesson. For young kids, a parent stays nearby on the beach.
  • Follow your instructor. If they think it isn't safe for you to go out, or to keep going, their call is final.

Wind, weather and changes

  • The wind forecast on this site is a forecast, not a promise. Taty and the crew decide on the day if conditions are right for your lesson.
  • If conditions aren't safe or right for your level, we move your lesson to another time.
  • Can't make it? Let us know on WhatsApp as early as you can, so someone else can take your spot.

Rentals

  • Gear is first come, first served. We don't reserve specific boards, sails or wings. A heads-up on WhatsApp helps us plan, but it isn't a reservation.
  • Rentals are for 1 hour, 2 hours, half a day or a full day. Anything longer you sort out with us on the beach.
  • Only rent what you can handle. If we think a board or sail size isn't right for you or for the wind, we'll suggest another.
  • Stay inside the area we show you on the beach, and come back in if the crew calls you in.
  • The gear is your responsibility while you have it. Bring it back clean of sand and on time. Tell us straight away about any damage or loss, so we can sort it out fairly together.

Gear storage

Storage is for your own gear, at the price shown on the rentals page. We keep an eye on the shack, but stored gear stays your responsibility. We advise you to have your own insurance for it.

Safety and risk

Windsurfing, wing foiling and wind foiling are water sports with real risks, such as falls, collisions, sunburn and getting tired out on the water. Our instructors and rescue boat are there to keep those risks small, but they can't take them away. By taking a lesson or renting gear, you accept these risks. Nothing in these terms limits what we owe you under the law if we are careless.

Look after yourself and others: wear sunscreen, drink water, and never go out alone without telling the crew.

Photos

We sometimes take photos and video on the beach to share online. If you'd rather not be in them, just tell us.

This website

We do our best to keep prices, times and information on this site correct, but mistakes happen. If something on the site doesn't match what we told you, what we confirm with you counts. The photos, videos and text on this site belong to Frans Paradise or the people who made them. Please ask before you reuse them.

Law

These terms follow the law of Bonaire (Caribbean Netherlands). If something goes wrong, talk to us first. Most things are easier to fix on the beach.
